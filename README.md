🎓 Campus Lost and Found Management System

A Campus Lost and Found Management System designed to help students and staff report, search for, and recover lost belongings within a college campus.

📌 About the Project

The Campus Lost and Found Management System is a project designed to make it easier to manage lost and found items in a college environment.

Students can report items they have lost or found, browse available reports, and identify belongings that may belong to them. The system aims to reduce the difficulty of recovering misplaced items on campus.

✨ Features

- 📦 Report lost items
- 🔎 Report found items
- 📋 View lost and found item details
- 🔍 Search for items
- 📍 Identify where an item was lost or found
- 🤝 Help connect lost items with their owners
- 🏫 Support a more organized campus lost-and-found process

⚙️ How It Works

1. A student reports a lost item with its details.
2. Another student or staff member reports a found item.
3. Users browse or search the available reports.
4. The possible owner identifies the matching item.
5. The item can be returned through an appropriate campus handover process.
